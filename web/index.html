Traffic signals

Cars drive between the houses of a small town, and traffic signals at the junctions take turns letting each direction through. Press Play to start the traffic; the line under the map says what is happening and the numbers below count how long trips are held up.

When "Change the map" is open, arrow keys move a cursor over the map and Enter or Space uses the chosen tool on the square under it. V, R, H and E pick the Select, Road, House and Erase tools. Plus and minus zoom, 0 shows the whole map, P plays or pauses.
